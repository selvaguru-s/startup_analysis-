💸 Tax Benefits: QSBS Explained

🎁 QSBS = Qualified Small Business Stock

Section 1202 of US tax code allows 100% exclusion of capital gains on qualified small business stock. This is one of the most powerful wealth-creation tools in US tax law.

Only available to US C-Corporation shareholders. India companies cannot provide this benefit.

QSBS Requirements

  • Must be US C-Corporation (Delaware qualifies ✅)
  • Hold stock for 5+ years
  • Company has less than $50M in assets when stock issued
  • Actively conducting business (not passive investment)
